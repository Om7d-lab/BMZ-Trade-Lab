# BMZ Trade Lab product brief

## Research baseline

TradeZella’s public materials identify the category baseline: a global dashboard with configurable P&L, account balance, win-rate, profit-factor, drawdown, calendar, recent-trade, and consistency widgets; trade and day views; rich notebook and templates; playbooks; filters and reports; replay/backtesting; mentor access; and AI-assisted reviews. See the official [homepage](https://www.tradezella.com/), [dashboard documentation](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7118437-understanding-dashboard-widgets-and-stats), [journal workflow](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/13863136-getting-started-with-tradezella), [notebook](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/7190696-organizing-and-managing-your-notes-within-your-tradezella-notebook), and [replay versus backtesting](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/11787298-what-is-the-trade-replay-feature-in-tradezella-and-how-does-it-compare-to-backtesting).

BMZ Trade Lab takes inspiration from the *problem space*, not TradeZella’s brand, layout, visuals, screenshots, or copy. Its differentiator is a calmer premium experience that turns preparation, execution, reflection, and evidence into one privacy-conscious loop.

## Product principles

1. Start useful with manual entry and reliable CSV mapping; never make users wait for a broker integration.
2. Separate executions, trades, notes, playbooks, rules, and reports so data remains explainable.
3. Treat privacy, tenancy, auditability, provider failures, and financial-risk disclaimers as first-class product work.
4. Never imply AI output is financial advice or allow it to submit an order.
5. Internationalize from day one: English default; Persian and Arabic RTL; other locales via message catalogs.

## Complete product map

- Marketing: conversion landing page, product, integrations, pricing, legal, and help content.
- Identity: secure auth, recovery, verification, profile, locale, timezone, organization membership, and entitlements.
- Journal core: accounts, trades, partial executions, tags, screenshots, notes, daily reviews, templates, filters, imports, and rollback/audit history.
- Analytics: dashboards, widget templates, privacy/unit modes, calendar, P&L/equity/drawdown, report builder, cross analysis, and exports.
- Discipline: playbooks, rule groups, trade rule checks, daily stages, heatmaps, streaks, and reminders.
- Advanced modules: broker/prop adapters, market data, replay, backtests, AI, mentorship, and billing.

## UI system

Graphite/navy foundations (`#07111D`, `#0C1827`), emerald (`#34D399`) and teal (`#2DD4BF`) action states, muted slate labels, rose loss states, substantial card radius, concise data-dense tables, keyboard-accessible controls, and restrained motion. The UI must show positive, negative, neutral, loading, empty, error, and permission-denied states for every data surface.
